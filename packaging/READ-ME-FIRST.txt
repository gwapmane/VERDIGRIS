VERDIGRIS  v0.1  -  Windows VST3 + Standalone

QUICK START (FL Studio)
1. Unzip this whole download (right-click > Extract All). Don't run things from inside the zip.
2. Double-click INSTALL.bat and click Yes when Windows asks.
   (It copies VERDIGRIS.vst3 into  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 , the folder FL Studio scans.)
3. Open FL Studio > Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins (scan).
4. Add VERDIGRIS to a mixer insert slot (it is an effect): click an empty slot, pick VERDIGRIS.

No install needed to just try it: double-click VERDIGRIS-Standalone.exe.

If Windows says "protected your PC": click More info > Run anyway (the plugin is not code-signed).

WHAT IT DOES
A fuzz pedal. The sound comes from a model of a two-transistor fuzz circuit (the kind in classic fuzz boxes), not a
simple distortion curve - so it cleans up when you turn your guitar down, sags when the battery is starved, and
reacts to how hard you play.

THE KNOBS
 FUZZ    how hard the transistors are pushed
 BIAS    where the transistors sit - low is spitty and gated, high is smooth and fat
 VOLUME  the output level (0 - 10)
THE TWO TOGGLES (bottom): TYPE (Ge = warm and woolly, Si = tight and fierce) and VOICING (FAT, ZONK, VIN).
THE SIX SIDE LEVERS (up = on). Flip them as fast as you like - they fade in and out with no clicks.
  Tap a lever and it stays. HOLD it (about a third of a second) and it is only on while you hold it.
  The small knob under each one sets how much:
   OCTAVE  adds a ringing octave up (BLEND)        STARVE  sags the battery so it dies and sputters (DEPTH)
   CHOP    chops the fuzz in time with your DAW (PATTERN)   CRUSH  crushes it into gritty digital fuzz (BITE)
   WASH    a big fuzzy reverb wash (MIX)           SWELL   the fuzz swells in after every note (TIME)
THE SMALL LEVER at the bottom left: A / LIVE / B - two memory slots for quick sound changes.
The footswitch: tap = fuzz on / off. HOLD = momentary.

IN THE PLUGIN
- Bar on top: < preset name >  (click the name for the list, factory presets + My Presets), Save, S/M/L window size,
  the up-arrow button hides the bar so only the pedal shows (tiny round handle top-left brings it back), gear = settings.
- The window in the middle is a little scope: the transfer curve (what goes in against what comes out), the waveform
  (dim = your guitar, bright = the fuzz), and the battery, Chop pattern and Swell.
- Settings > LOOK: body colour, lights colour, texture (hammered, crinkle, flake, brushed), your own picture,
  your own video / GIF / picture in the window (GIF and pictures anywhere; MP4/MOV/WMV/AVI on Windows).
- Settings > SOUND: extras for the side levers, tempo, tone / mids / gate / battery and other "under the hood" controls,
  Ramp (the knobs move by themselves), levels, and MIDI learn.
- MIDI knobs: right-click any knob, lever or the footswitch on the pedal, then move a knob on your controller.
  Or use FL's own way: plugin menu > Parameters > Link all parameters.
